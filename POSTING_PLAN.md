# Posting plan

Everything to post, in order. Each line says which file to upload and where its text is.
Long videos go up on **Mondays**. One Short per day in between.

How to post a long video: upload the mp4, then copy title, description, tags and settings from its `upload/youtube.md`, upload `upload/thumbnail.jpg`, add the end screen (Subscribe on the dashed circle, a video on the dashed frame, last 10 seconds), then post and pin the comment from `youtube.md`.

How to post a Short: upload the mp4, copy title, description and tags from `upload/shorts.md`, tap **Related video** and pick that week's long video, then pin the comment. Replace `LINK` with the long video's URL. Max 2 hashtags besides #shorts.

Video files are not in this repo (too big). They were sent in the Claude chat; keep them saved on your phone or Drive.
